I am an undergraduate student at Tsinghua University, Weiyang College, studying Mathematics and Physics + Mechanical Engineering. I expect to graduate in 2028. You can reach me at nyk24@mails.tsinghua.edu.cn (academic) or o14554494@gmail.com (personal).

My research interests lie in robot learning and manipulation, particularly efficient and reliable visuomotor imitation learning. I am also interested in reinforcement learning, policy adaptation and generalization, and vision-language-action models for long-horizon manipulation.

I conduct robotics research in Prof. Huazhe Xu’s group at Tsinghua University. My work on AdaTempo involved demonstration processing, policy training, simulation experiments, and real-robot evaluation.
